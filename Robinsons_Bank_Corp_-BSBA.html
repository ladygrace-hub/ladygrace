Company Overview

Name of the Company

Robinsons Bank Corporation (RBank)

Background & History

Robinsons Bank Corporation, also known as RBank, was the financial services arm of the JG Summit Group of Companies, one of the fastest growing commercial banks in the Philippines in terms of capitalization and asset size. The bank was 60.0% owned by JG Summit Capital Services Corp. and 40.0% owned by Robinsons Retail Holdings, Inc.

Robinsons Bank started as a savings bank in November 1997 amid the backdrop of the Asian financial crisis. In 2002, Robinsons Savings Bank acquired the branches of ABN Amro Savings Bank (Philippines), its licenses to operate the branches and bank deposit portfolio. This acquisition made Robinsons Savings Bank the seventh largest thrift bank during that period.

In February 2010, Robinsons Savings Bank acquired the controlling interest of the Royal Bank of Scotland (Philippines). In August of the same year, Royal Bank of Scotland (Philippines) was renamed as Robinsons Bank Corporation. Then by December, Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) approved the merger of Robinsons Savings Bank and Robinsons Bank Corporation, with Robinsons Bank Corporation as the surviving entity.

In 2012, BSP approved the Bank's move to acquire Legazpi Savings Bank (LSB). The acquisition of LSB opened up business lines and grew the target market for Robinsons Bank in the Bicol region. LSB became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Robinsons Bank.

On September 30, 2022, Robinsons Bank together with the Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI) disclosed their merger expected to complete by 2023, with BPI as the surviving entity. The merger took effect on January 1, 2024, with BPI targeting to complete its integration with the remaining branches of Robinsons Bank by October 2025.

Operational/Financial Highlights

Prior to its merger with BPI, Robinsons Bank demonstrated consistent growth in its financial performance over the years.

The bank's financial performance was shaped by strategic initiatives under its Roadmap 2020, which focused on capacity building and core income growth. In 2015, the bank rolled out this strategic plan with an initial phase of capacity building. The second phase, focused on core income growth, commenced in 2016.

By 2017, the bank significantly expanded its operations by oversubscribing its first Long-term Negotiable Certificates of Deposits (LTNCD) of Php4.1 billion. The launch of credit card products in the same year marked its entry into the credit card business.

In 2020, the Bank launched a new 5-year strategic initiative, Roadmap 2024, which aimed to position the Bank among the Top 11-13 Philippine banks in terms of assets by 2024 via organic growth.

Organizational Structure

The bank maintained a robust organizational structure that supported its strategic objectives and ensured proper governance and risk management.

The Board of Directors was the highest governing body, overseeing various committees including Executive Committee, Corporate Governance Committee, Risk Oversight Committee, Audit Committee, Trust Committee, Related Party Transactions Committee, and IT Steering Committee.

The Senior Management team, led by the President and CEO, was responsible for the day-to-day operations and implementation of the bank's strategy.

Capital Structure and Adequacy

Robinsons Bank maintained a strong capital position, exceeding the minimum requirements set by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) and international standards.

Major Shareholders

  • JG Summit Capital Services Corp.: 60%
  • Robinsons Retail Holdings, Inc.: 40%

The bank's solid capital structure allowed it to support business growth while maintaining financial stability. As a subsidiary of JG Summit Holdings, the bank benefited from the conglomerate's strong financial backing.
