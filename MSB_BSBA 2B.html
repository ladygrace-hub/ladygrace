History & Background

Malayan Savings Bank was established in 1996 as a savings and mortgage bank in the Philippines. Since then, it has built a reputation as a reliable financial institution offering a wide range of balanced financial services. The bank celebrated its 25th anniversary with a fresh look and new tagline, continuing its commitment to serving Filipino families and businesses.

As its full name "Malayan Bank Savings and Mortgage Bank, Inc." indicates, the bank's main focus is on personal savings and mortgage products. With 21 branches located across the Philippines, Malayan Bank is positioned to meet the needs of Filipinos nationwide.

The bank operates under the regulation of the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), the central monetary authority of the Philippines. MSB is committed to maintaining a respected position as one of the country's top savings banks in terms of stability and strength.

As a savings bank regulated by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), Malayan Savings Bank must maintain capital adequacy ratios that meet or exceed regulatory requirements. The bank's capital structure typically consists of high-quality capital components to support business growth while providing protection against potential risks.

BSP Capital Requirements for Banks

The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas sets minimum capital requirements for all banks operating in the Philippines, including:

  • Minimum Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) requirements
  • Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio requirements
  • Tier 1 capital ratio requirements
  • Leverage ratio requirements

Banks like Malayan Savings Bank typically maintain capital levels above these regulatory minimums to ensure financial stability and support their business operations.

Components of Bank Capital

A bank's capital structure generally includes:

  • Paid-up Capital: Direct investments from shareholders
  • Retained Earnings: Accumulated profits not distributed to shareholders
  • Reserves: Funds set aside for specific purposes or to strengthen the bank's financial position
  • Other Capital Instruments: Additional capital components that may be included based on regulatory guidelines

Corporate Social Responsibility

At Malayan Savings Bank, we are committed to making a positive impact in the communities we serve. Our corporate social responsibility initiatives focus on education, financial inclusion, environmental sustainability, community development, and disaster relief.

Key CSR Initiatives

Education

MSB Financial Literacy Program

A comprehensive program that provides financial education to students, young adults, and underserved communities. The initiative includes school workshops, online learning resources, and community seminars.

Impact

Reached over 100,000 Malaysians, with surveys showing a 65% increase in understanding of basic financial concepts.

Environmental Sustainability

Green Banking Initiative

A multi-faceted program that includes paperless banking, energy-efficient branches, and preferential financing for green projects and businesses.

Impact

Reduced carbon footprint by 30% over five years, planted 50,000 trees, and financed renewable energy projects generating over 25MW of clean energy.

Financial Inclusion

Community Entrepreneurship Fund

A microfinancing program that provides small loans, business training, and mentorship to entrepreneurs from underserved communities, with a special focus on women and youth entrepreneurs.

Impact

Supported over 5,000 micro-entrepreneurs, creating an estimated 12,000 jobs in local communities and improving household incomes by an average of 40%.

Humanitarian Aid

MSB Disaster Relief Fund

A rapid response fund that provides immediate financial and logistical support to communities affected by natural disasters such as floods, landslides, and other emergencies.

Impact

Provided emergency assistance to over 25,000 individuals affected by disasters in the past five years, including temporary housing, food supplies, and rebuilding support.

Technology Access

Digital Inclusion for Seniors

A program designed to help senior citizens navigate digital banking platforms safely and effectively through hands-on training sessions, simplified digital tools, and dedicated support services.

Impact

Trained over 10,000 seniors in digital banking skills, resulting in a 45% increase in digital banking adoption among participants aged 65 and above.
